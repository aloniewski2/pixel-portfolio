# Project: CampusFi
Summary: Concept and product spec for a student finance app — where money goes, cashflow around the academic year, and a safer intro to crypto. Written up, not yet built: this repo is the spec.
Code: https://github.com/aloniewski2/CampusFi
Last updated: 2026-08-18

Problem: Students juggle rent, dining, books, fees, rides, and side-hustle income—with temptations to dabble in crypto. Existing tools feel generic.

Solution: CampusFi unifies banking insights + student‑centric budgets + beginner‑friendly crypto tracking. An AI advisor personalizes insights, forecasts upcoming cash crunches, and surfaces what’s trending among peers (categories and crypto narratives) in a privacy‑safe way.

1‑Line Pitch: “Your campus money HQ—with smart budgets, safe crypto, and AI that knows what students will care about next.”
